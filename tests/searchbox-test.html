History

Game playing programs have been used since the 1950s to demonstrate and test AI's most advanced techniques. Deep Blue became the first computer chess-playing system to beat a reigning world chess champion, Garry Kasparov, on 11 May 1997.

The field of AI research was founded at a workshop at Dartmouth College in 1956. The attendees became the leaders of AI research in the 1960s.

Energy

Power consumption by AI is responsible for an increase in fossil fuel use, and a ChatGPT search involves the use of 10 times the electrical energy as a Google search.
